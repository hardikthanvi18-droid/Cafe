Privacy Policy

At Sip And Joy, we respect and prioritize your personal privacy. This privacy policy explains what personal information we collect, how it is used to deliver your orders, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you explicitly provide, such as your name, delivery address, phone number, and email address when placing orders or registering a customer account.

2. Use of Information

Your details are used strictly to process orders, communicate preparation/delivery updates, and provide optional marketing newsletters if you opt in.

3. Security

We use password hashing (Werkzeug / scrypt) and never store raw passwords or sensitive card credentials.
